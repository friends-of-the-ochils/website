intro: 'Friends of the Ochils was founded in November 1992, after our founder Rennie McOwan asked in *The Herald* whether it was time someone set one up. Read [the origin of FotO](/about-us/the-origin-of-foto/).


  This archive gathers our past newsletters, annual reports and campaign papers so that the story of more than thirty years of looking after the Ochils isn''t lost.'
appeal: 'Our archive has gaps, and we''d love your help to fill them. We''re looking for:


  - **Newsletters 1 to 38**, from the 1990s and 2000s. Our earliest copy is issue 39, from spring 2011.

  - **Any newsletters from 2016 to 2020**, if any were produced.

  - **Annual reviews** from any year other than 2008–09, 2009–10 and 2020–21.

  - Press cuttings, photos, letters and AGM papers about FotO and the Ochils.


  If you have something in a drawer or a loft, please [get in touch](/contact-us/). We''ll scan it carefully and give it back.'
scanning_tips: 'If you''d like to scan something yourself:


  - Scan at **300 dpi**, in colour if the original has any colour, otherwise greyscale.

  - Save it as a **PDF**, one file per document, with the pages in order.

  - If your scanner can, make it a **searchable PDF** (sometimes called OCR).

  - Try to keep each file **under 10 MB**.

  - Tell us roughly when it''s from and who lent it, so we can credit you.'
