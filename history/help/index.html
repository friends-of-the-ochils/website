Help us fill the gaps

Our archive has gaps, and we'd love your help to fill them. We're looking for:

  • Newsletters 1 to 38, from the 1990s and 2000s. Our earliest copy is issue 39, from spring 2011.
  • Any newsletters from 2016 to 2020, if any were produced.
  • Annual reviews from any year other than 2008–09, 2009–10 and 2020–21.
  • Press cuttings, photos, letters and AGM papers about FotO and the Ochils.

If you have something in a drawer or a loft, please get in touch. We'll scan it carefully and give it back.

Scanning tips

If you'd like to scan something yourself:

  • Scan at 300 dpi, in colour if the original has any colour, otherwise greyscale.
  • Save it as a PDF, one file per document, with the pages in order.
  • If your scanner can, make it a searchable PDF (sometimes called OCR).
  • Try to keep each file under 10 MB.
  • Tell us roughly when it's from and who lent it, so we can credit you.
